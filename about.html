About Adam Operations Group.

AOG helps business owners build stronger, more organized businesses that do not depend on them for every decision.

Founded by Adam Broussard, AOG works with established, owner-led businesses on operations, leadership, systems, and sustainable growth.

Many owners reach a point where they are the center of every decision, problem, and responsibility. That makes it difficult to grow, to delegate, or to step away from daily operations.

The work is structured processes, accountability, efficiency, and teams that can operate with less owner involvement. The mission is the shift from working in the business to working on it.

Build a business that runs without you.

That is the standard. Not a thinner calendar for a week. A company with systems, leaders, and a way of operating that does not collapse when the owner steps out of a decision.

Adam Broussard is the founder of Adam Operations Group. He is a Navy veteran, a Louisiana real estate broker, an entrepreneur, and an operator.

He has spent more than 20 years building, managing, and restructuring local service businesses. That work runs through real estate, construction, property management, acquisitions, financing, and day-to-day operations.

He is the author of Adapt or Be Managed, on the realities of ownership, leadership, operational problems, and the need for systems that can last. The approach at AOG comes from that experience: practical fixes that hold up in a real operation.
