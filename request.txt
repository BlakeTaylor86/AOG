About Adam Operations Group
Adam Operations Group helps business owners build stronger, more organized businesses that don't depend on them for every decision.

Founded by Adam Broussard, AOG focuses on helping established, owner-led businesses improve their operations, develop stronger leadership, implement effective systems, and create sustainable growth.

Many business owners reach a point where they become the center of every decision, problem, and responsibility within their company. This can make it difficult to grow, delegate responsibilities, or step away from daily operations.

Adam Operations Group works to solve these challenges by helping owners create structured processes, establish accountability, improve efficiency, and develop teams capable of operating independently.

The overall mission is to help business owners transition from constantly working in their businesses to effectively working on their businesses.

Main Message: Build a Business That Runs Without You.

What Adam Operations Group Does
Adam Operations Group provides business operations consulting, leadership development, systems implementation, and strategic guidance for established businesses.

Its primary areas of focus include:

Business Operations & Systems Development

Helping businesses create organized, repeatable processes that improve efficiency and consistency. This includes developing standard operating procedures (SOPs), documenting workflows, identifying operational weaknesses, and establishing systems that allow businesses to operate more effectively.

Leadership Development & Delegation

Helping business owners establish clear roles, responsibilities, and expectations within their teams. AOG focuses on developing stronger managers, improving employee accountability, and giving leadership teams the structure and authority needed to make decisions without constant owner involvement.

Business Performance & Financial Visibility

Helping owners better understand their business performance through operational scorecards, performance tracking, measurable goals, and improved financial visibility. The focus is on identifying inefficiencies and giving business owners the information they need to make better decisions.

Automation, AI & Technology Integration

Helping businesses implement technology that simplifies daily operations, reduces repetitive tasks, improves communication, and increases productivity. This can include workflow automation, AI tools, and integrating technology into existing business processes.

Business Growth & Operational Strategy

Helping businesses develop the structure needed to grow sustainably. AOG works to identify operational bottlenecks, improve scalability, strengthen management systems, and reduce the owner's involvement in everyday decisions.

The Adam Operating System (AOS)
Adam Operations Group uses a five-step framework called the Adam Operating System, designed to help business owners identify operational weaknesses and build stronger businesses.

The five components include:

Awareness: Understanding where operational problems exist and identifying the areas that need improvement.

Documentation: Creating clear procedures, expectations, and repeatable processes.

Delegation: Establishing responsibilities and giving employees and managers the authority to make decisions.

Automation: Using technology to reduce repetitive tasks and improve operational efficiency.

Leadership: Developing capable leaders, improving accountability, and establishing a stronger business culture.

This framework helps businesses become more organized, efficient, scalable, and less dependent on their owners.

Who Adam Operations Group Helps
Adam Operations Group primarily works with established, owner-led businesses that already have employees, customers, and ongoing operations but need stronger systems and leadership structures.

These are often business owners who:

Feel overwhelmed by daily responsibilities.

Are responsible for nearly every important decision.

Struggle with delegation or employee accountability.

Have inconsistent or undocumented business processes.

Want to expand but lack the operational structure to support growth.

Spend too much time solving daily problems instead of focusing on the future.

Want more freedom and flexibility without sacrificing business performance.

AOG helps these owners identify what's holding their businesses back and implement practical solutions.

Operator Exposure Assessment

A more detailed evaluation of a company's operational structure, leadership responsibilities, decision-making processes, and owner dependency.

90-Day Adam Operating System Implementation

A structured implementation process focused on developing stronger business systems, documented procedures, leadership accountability, and operational independence.

Ongoing Operational Support

Continued guidance to help business owners maintain their systems, monitor performance, improve operations, and support long-term growth.
