The Adam Operating System

A five-step framework for finding operational weaknesses and building a business that is more organized, efficient, scalable, and less dependent on its owner.

  1. 01

    Awareness

    See where the operational problems are, and which areas need work first.

  2. 02

    Documentation

    Write the procedures, expectations, and repeatable processes the business should own.

  3. 03

    Delegation

    Set responsibilities and give people the authority to decide without the owner.

  4. 04

    Automation

    Use technology to reduce repetitive work and make daily operations more efficient.

  5. 05

    Leadership

    Develop capable leaders, raise accountability, and build a stronger culture.

Common questions

With the Operator Exposure Scorecard. It is free, takes about eight minutes, and does not require an account. The result is a score, the operational areas creating the most drag, and a clearer first step.
