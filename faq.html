Common questions.

Short answers about the work, who it is for, and how an engagement starts.

What does Adam Operations Group do?

AOG helps established, owner-led businesses put operations, leadership, systems, and accountability in place so the company does not depend on the owner for every decision. That includes documented processes, performance visibility, practical technology, and a structure that can support growth.

Who is it for?

Owners who already have employees, customers, and a working business, and who are still the center of the decisions and the daily problems. The fit is a company that needs structure, not one that is just starting.

What is the Adam Operating System?

A five-step framework: Awareness, Documentation, Delegation, Automation, and Leadership. It is how AOG finds operational weaknesses and builds a business that is more organized and less dependent on its owner. See the five steps.

What is the Operator Exposure Scorecard?

A free, 30-question diagnostic. It evaluates five areas, returns an Operator Exposure Score, highlights weaknesses, and points to opportunities. It takes about eight minutes and does not require an account. A full report is sent to your inbox. Read how it works.

How does the work start?

With the scorecard. After that, the ways to continue are a 5-day bottleneck challenge, a deeper Operator Exposure Assessment, a 90-day implementation of the operating system, or ongoing operational support.

What is the 5-Day Owner Bottleneck Challenge?

A focused challenge to identify one major operational bottleneck and begin a practical solution.

What is the 90-day implementation?

A structured build of systems, documented procedures, leadership accountability, and more operational independence.

Who is Adam Broussard?

The founder of Adam Operations Group. He is a Navy veteran, a Louisiana real estate broker, and an operator with more than 20 years building, managing, and restructuring local service businesses. He wrote Adapt or Be Managed. More about Adam.
